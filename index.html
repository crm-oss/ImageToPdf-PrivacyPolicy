Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 18 May 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Image to PDF Converter, package name in.ladam.imagetopdf, developed by Retcon.

1. Overview

Image to PDF Converter allows users to select images, capture photos, create PDF files, save PDFs on their device, open PDFs, share PDFs, and manage saved files.

The app is designed to work locally on the user's device. Images, PDFs, and selected files are processed on the device and are not uploaded to our servers.

2. Data We Collect

The app does not collect, upload, sell, or share personal data, images, PDFs, documents, or files with the developer.

The app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash reporting SDKs, login systems, or backend/server upload functionality in the current version.

3. Permissions Used

Camera Permission

The app may request camera access when you choose to capture a photo for PDF creation. Camera access is used only for this purpose.

Photos and Media Permission

The app may request access to photos or media so you can select images from your device and convert them into PDF files. Selected images are processed locally on your device.

File and Storage Access

The app may use Android file picker or storage access features to let you choose files, save PDFs, open PDFs, share PDFs, or delete files. Files are stored locally on your device.

4. Local Storage

The app may store basic information locally on your device, such as recent file paths, favorite file paths, and app preferences such as dark mode. This information is stored only on your device and is not sent to us.

5. Sharing PDFs

When you choose to share a PDF, the app opens the Android share sheet. You choose which external app or service receives the file. Once you share a file with another app or service, that third party's own privacy policy applies.

6. Feedback and Feature Requests

If you choose to send feedback or request a feature, the app opens your email app with our contact email address pre-filled. We receive only the information you choose to send by email, such as your email address and message content.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Deletion

Since the app does not store user data on our servers, there is no server-side account data to delete. PDFs and files created by the app are stored locally on your device and can be deleted by you from within the app or through your device file manager.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
